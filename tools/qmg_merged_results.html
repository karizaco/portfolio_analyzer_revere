How to read this. A date's row pools every log that date has in a run, exactly as the replay tool does, and scores against the ground truth's union over that date's captures. The red tickers are holdings the video never had; the amber ones are holdings it did have and the scan missed. The purple column names the tickers the swallowed-holding rule published alongside a cluster's canonical.

This is a surviving sample. Only dates a probe log exists for appear here, and only the runs listed are counted. Every number here is a measurement of the scans on disk — not of the pipeline as a whole.
