Color coding: Green = GT ticker correctly detected. Red dashed = GT ticker MISSED. Orange = false positive (detected but not in GT).

Three parser configurations compared:

  • Single-capture legacy — accept any ticker-shape token in the seed lexicon.
  • Single-capture column-aware — only accept ticker-shape words that land in the dominant ticker column AND have a price token to their RIGHT on the same line.
  • Multi-frame merge (≥2/3 captures) — accept tickers that survive across multiple captures of the same video. Dramatically reduces FPs from single-frame OCR garbling.
